# THE HER × OMID — PROJECT HANDOFF

**Project:** Cinematic bilingual collaboration proposal  
**Primary output:** Editorial, scroll-driven landing page  
**Default language:** English  
**Secondary language:** Persian / RTL  
**Visual direction:** Editorial Cinematic / Quiet Luxury  
**Brand balance:** 70% The Her / 30% Omid  
**Workflow:** GitHub → Arena Agent + Google AI Studio → Google Flow →
Netlify

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. PROJECT NORTH STAR

This is **not** a normal PDF proposal, portfolio, or services page.

The landing page itself must be the first proof of Omid’s ability to
think strategically, diagnose business problems, design digital
experiences, use data and marketing intelligently, and apply AI where it
creates real leverage.

The visitor should not leave thinking:

> “Here is a marketer with many services.”

They should leave thinking:

> “This person can become a flexible growth and digital partner for The
> Her and its clients.”

### Core positioning

**Omid connects AI, data, marketing and digital experiences into one
growth engine.**

### Partnership positioning

**The Her understands the woman behind the business.  
I focus on the system behind the business.**

### Core narrative

**Different businesses. Different problems. One partner. Many
possibilities.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The proposal should establish a flexible partnership with two modes.

## A. The Her Growth Partner

Omid can support The Her itself across:

- Growth strategy
- Lead generation
- Funnel design
- Performance marketing
- SEO
- CRO
- Analytics / tracking / attribution
- Website / landing pages
- UX / UI
- Digital products / apps
- AI workflows
- Automation

## B. The Her Client Growth / Digital Partner

The Her can bring Omid into client projects when deeper digital, growth,
data, product, website or AI capability is needed.

Possible formats:

- Visible collaboration
- Project-based specialist
- White-label delivery
- Behind-the-scenes execution
- End-to-end digital/growth workstream

The value proposition is:

> **The Her can offer broader capabilities to its clients without
> needing to build every capability internally.**

# 4. BRAND & ART DIRECTION

Based on the screenshots supplied for The Her, the visual language is:

- Quiet luxury
- Editorial
- Feminine
- Aspirational
- Lifestyle-oriented
- Warm
- Minimal
- High whitespace
- Elegant typography
- Large imagery
- Restrained, intentional motion

Visual palette direction:

- warm ivory
- champagne / beige
- warm taupe
- dark chocolate / espresso
- soft black

Typography:

- large editorial headlines
- thin / elegant weights
- uppercase English headlines where appropriate
- generous letter spacing
- strong whitespace
- minimal UI

### Critical constraint

Do **not** turn this into a generic:

- AI landing page
- SaaS website
- futuristic tech site
- neon/cyber interface
- over-animated portfolio

AI should feel like **intelligence inside the system**, not the visual
identity.

# 5. EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLE

The website should feel like a short editorial film that happens to be a
website.

The scroll is the narrative.

Motion should be:

- slow
- intentional
- elegant
- cinematic
- meaningful

Avoid:

- animation for animation’s sake
- excessive parallax
- aggressive transitions
- visual noise
- unnecessary UI

Desired emotional sequence:

**Curiosity → Recognition → Tension → Understanding → Possibility →
Trust → Action**

# 7. HYPOTHETICAL CLIENT CASES

These are intentionally **hypothetical examples**. Never present them as
real The Her clients.

The purpose is to demonstrate Omid’s way of thinking.

## CASE 01 — SALES

> **“We have traffic. But not enough sales.”**

### Diagnose

The problem may not be traffic.

Potential issues:

- funnel friction
- offer / messaging
- landing page
- trust
- CTA
- tracking
- conversion path

### Possible response

- Funnel analysis
- Tracking / attribution
- Landing page optimization
- CRO
- Messaging
- Testing

### Key line

> **Growth doesn’t always need more traffic. Sometimes it needs a better
> system.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CASE 02 — DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

> **“Our business is growing. But our digital presence isn’t.”**

### Diagnose

The brand has evolved, but the digital experience has not.

Potential issues:

- outdated website
- weak mobile experience
- unclear UX
- inconsistent UI
- poor conversion journey

### Possible response

**Strategy → UX → UI → Development → Measurement**

### Key line

> **A website shouldn’t just represent the business. It should move the
> business forward.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CASE 03 — AI / OPERATIONS

> **“We’re growing. But everything still depends on people.”**

### Diagnose

Potential issues:

- manual follow-up
- fragmented data
- repetitive reporting
- slow operations
- inefficient content workflows

### Possible response

- AI workflows
- automation
- CRM/data connections
- intelligent reporting
- workflow redesign

### Key line

> **AI shouldn’t replace the business. It should make the business more
> capable.**

## PHASE 01 — THE HER INTELLIGENCE

### Objective

Build a reliable understanding of The Her before designing the proposal.

### Tasks

1.  Review all provided The Her screenshots.
2.  Review available public material.
3.  Map visual language.
4.  Map apparent audience.
5.  Map visible offers.
6.  Identify brand strengths.
7.  Identify digital opportunities.
8.  Separate facts from hypotheses.
9.  Map potential client-service opportunities.
10. Map Omid’s complementary capabilities.

### Critical rule

Internal The Her data is not currently available.

Therefore assumptions must be written as:

- Potential opportunity
- Hypothesis
- Worth validating
- If current acquisition is primarily social-led…
- A possible funnel opportunity…

Never state an assumption as an internal fact.

**The Her Intelligence Report**

partnership fit.
