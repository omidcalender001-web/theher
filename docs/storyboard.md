Scene 1 — The Vision

Scene: The Vision — Act I: A world of business women

Purpose: Establish The Her’s world before introducing Omid. The audience should first feel the brand, not understand the partnership.

Narrative line: A WORLD OF BUSINESS WOMEN.

Visual: A sequence of quiet editorial portraits: a woman preparing for her day, entering an elegant workspace, reviewing notes, walking through a city, sitting in conversation. Warm ivory, champagne and espresso tones. No visible technology as the subject.

Motion: Slow cinematic parallax. Images drift at different depths while typography appears with restrained vertical movement. 700–1200ms easing: cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1).

Transition: The final portrait holds slightly longer. The image gradually gives way to a warm ivory field while the headline remains for one final beat.

Interaction: Scroll controls the reveal. Each scroll movement introduces another fragment of The Her’s world rather than triggering abrupt scene changes.

Audio: Very subtle room tone, distant city ambience, soft fabric movement. Optional restrained cinematic score entering only near the end.

Asset required: 4–6 editorial female portraits; one hero image; subtle film-grain texture; The Her wordmark; ambient sound bed.

Mobile fallback: One full-bleed portrait with a slow Ken Burns-style movement. Headline remains fixed within the visual hierarchy; supporting imagery becomes a vertical sequence.

Scene 2 — The Business

Scene: The Business — Act II: Behind every ambitious woman, a different business

Purpose: Move from the woman to what she is building. Establish individuality and create the need for a tailored approach.

Narrative line: Behind every ambitious woman, there is a different business.

Visual: One woman remains the visual anchor while the environment subtly changes around her: brand materials, product, laptop, studio, meeting table, packaging, website interface. The business world emerges without becoming a conventional montage.

Motion: The central portrait stays almost still while surrounding layers slowly enter, scale and shift. The visual becomes progressively more complex as the scroll continues.

Transition: Individual layers dissolve into one composed frame, then typography isolates the thought: Different ambitions create different businesses.

Interaction: Scroll progressively reveals the “business behind the woman.” A subtle cursor or hover interaction may reveal contextual fragments, but nothing should feel like a software demo.

Audio: Soft environmental textures: paper, room ambience, distant keyboard sounds. No obvious digital sound effects.

Asset required: 1–2 hero portraits; 4–6 business-context image/video fragments; abstract editorial overlays; subtle paper/product textures.

Mobile fallback: Portrait first, followed by three vertically stacked business fragments. Reduce simultaneous layers to preserve clarity and performance.

Scene 3 — The Complexity

Scene: The Complexity — Act III: Different businesses. Different problems.

Purpose: Introduce complexity and challenge the assumption that every business needs the same growth solution.

Narrative line: Different businesses. Different problems.

Visual: Seven problem statements appear one by one in a large editorial typographic field:

MORE SALES
MORE LEADS
BETTER CONVERSION
A BETTER WEBSITE
BETTER DATA
AI & AUTOMATION
A DIGITAL PRODUCT

Each begins alone, then joins a growing composition.

Motion: Each statement enters with restrained vertical movement and slight opacity change. Earlier statements remain visible, creating intentional visual accumulation rather than rapid replacement.

Transition: At peak density, the seven statements freeze. Everything fades except one question:

“So why should they all receive the same solution?”

Interaction: Scroll determines the order and pacing. The user should feel the increasing complexity before receiving the question.

Audio: Minimal low-frequency room tone. Each new problem can have an almost imperceptible tonal shift, never a click or notification sound.

Asset required: Typography system; seven problem labels; editorial background texture; optional macro footage of paper, fabric or architectural surfaces.

Mobile fallback: Convert the seven problems into a clean vertical editorial list. Reveal them in groups of two or three, ending with the question as a full-screen statement.

Scene 4 — The System

Scene: The System — Act V: The partnership — The Her = the woman / Omid = the system

Purpose: Introduce Omid as a complementary capability, not as a replacement for The Her’s identity or expertise.

Narrative line: The Her understands the woman. Omid focuses on the system.

Visual: Split composition. On one side: The Her — woman, identity, ambition, business, vision. On the other: Omid — growth, data, marketing, experience, AI. Both sides retain the same editorial language.

Motion: The two worlds begin independently. As the scroll progresses, their boundaries soften and the two compositions gradually move toward one another.

Transition: The vertical divide disappears. Both sides settle into one shared frame with a single statement:

ONE PARTNERSHIP. TWO COMPLEMENTARY PERSPECTIVES.

Interaction: Scroll creates the convergence. Optional hover/tap on each side reveals its five supporting concepts without changing the main composition.

Audio: The ambient sound from The Her’s world continues. A second subtle tonal layer enters as Omid appears. The two layers resolve into one restrained soundscape.

Asset required: The Her imagery; Omid visual identity; typographic system; abstract system/data textures; optional portrait of Omid or restrained personal mark.

Mobile fallback: Replace the split-screen with sequential cards: THE WOMAN → THE SYSTEM → THE PARTNERSHIP. The final frame combines both.

Scene 5 — The Growth Engine

Scene: The Growth Engine — Act VI: DATA + MARKETING + EXPERIENCE + AI → one engine

Purpose: Make the partnership tangible. Show that Omid connects disciplines rather than presenting isolated services.

Narrative line: DATA + MARKETING + EXPERIENCE + AI → ONE GROWTH ENGINE.

Visual: Four elegant typographic pillars arranged around an understated central point:

DATA
MARKETING
EXPERIENCE
AI

Thin lines connect them toward the center. The visual should feel closer to editorial information design than a technology diagram.

Motion: Each discipline enters independently. The connecting lines appear only when their relationship becomes narratively relevant. The four elements slowly converge into one central typographic lockup: ONE GROWTH ENGINE.

Transition: The abstract engine dissolves into a real business journey: attention → interaction → conversion → retention → growth.

Interaction: Scroll progressively activates each component. Hover/tap can reveal one concise role for each discipline.

Audio: A restrained tonal pulse builds as the four elements connect, then resolves into silence at ONE GROWTH ENGINE.

Asset required: Motion typography; vector connection lines; subtle data texture; optional abstract macro footage; no conventional dashboards, holograms or glowing interfaces.

Mobile fallback: Four vertically stacked disciplines converge toward a single central statement. Replace simultaneous animation with sequential activation.

Scene 6 — The Collaboration

Scene: The Collaboration — Partnership value

Purpose: Turn the conceptual partnership into a practical business model and answer: How could The Her actually use this capability?

Narrative line: What if The Her could offer more — without becoming more?

Visual: Three editorial pathways emerge from one central THE HER node:

DIRECT
The Her ↔ Omid

CLIENT
The Her + Omid + Client

WHITE LABEL
The Her → Client / Omid → Behind the scenes

Each pathway is represented through restrained typography and subtle connective movement rather than corporate diagrams.

Motion: The three models reveal sequentially. The central The Her position remains fixed while each collaboration path expands outward and returns to the center.

Transition: The three paths collapse into one statement:

AN EXTENDED DIGITAL CAPABILITY, AVAILABLE WHEN THE BUSINESS NEEDS IT.

Interaction: Each model can be clicked/tapped to expand one short description. Opening one model gently pushes the others aside rather than opening a modal.

Audio: Return to the warmer ambient soundscape of Scene 1. The partnership should feel human and practical, not technological.

Asset required: Three collaboration diagrams; refined typography; subtle connector animations; optional The Her client-project imagery.

Mobile fallback: Three full-width stacked panels. Tap expands each model inline. No horizontal carousel unless necessary.

Scene 7 — The Future

Scene: The Future — CTA

Purpose: Resolve the narrative emotionally and invite a conversation without turning the ending into a conventional sales pitch.

Narrative line: Let’s explore what we could build together.

Visual: Return to the visual language of Scene 1, but now with greater openness: an elegant architectural space, a woman walking toward natural light, or an understated editorial horizon. The Her × Omid appears with generous negative space.

Motion: Almost imperceptible forward movement. Typography arrives slowly, with the final line appearing only after the visual has settled. The last frame should breathe.

Transition: No hard transition. The page gradually becomes quieter, ending on warm ivory with the final partnership lockup.

Interaction: One primary CTA:

START A CONVERSATION

Hover: subtle letter-spacing expansion and a restrained underline movement. On mobile: gentle press state, no exaggerated animation.

Audio: The score resolves into a single soft sustained tone, then fades naturally. Silence after the CTA appears.

Asset required: Final editorial hero image or cinematic video loop; The Her × Omid lockup; CTA treatment; subtle grain; final sound cue.

Mobile fallback: Full-screen portrait/video with centered headline and CTA. Keep the final frame visually sparse, with generous vertical space and no secondary navigation competing with the invitation.
